Leave and meetings, in your menu bar.

Kakitangan Companion watches your team's Kakitangan leave and your Google Calendar, then tells you before it matters.

Download for macOS Privacy policy

Two feeds your day actually depends on.

It runs quietly on your Mac, checks on a schedule you set, and only speaks up with Time Sensitive notifications.

Who is on leave

Sign in with one or more Kakitangan accounts, pick the people you manage, and get a weekday summary of who is away in the days ahead.

Meetings beside leave

Connect Google Calendar to see upcoming meetings next to the leave list, with reminders at the lead times you choose.

Cancellations, caught early

While the app is open it watches your primary calendar and alerts you when a meeting is cancelled. You can answer invitations from the same card.

Menu bar, Dock, or both

Choose how visible the app is, let it open at login, and keep it running after the window closes. Updates arrive in app through Sparkle.

Your data stays on your Mac.

There is no Kakitangan Companion server. The app talks directly to Kakitangan and to Google, and keeps what it needs locally.

Kakitangan credentials

Your account password is stored in the macOS Keychain. Only the account email, the people you monitor, and your schedule settings are saved in app preferences.

Google Calendar access

The app asks for permission to read events on your primary calendar and to update your own RSVP. Tokens are held by Google Sign-In on your Mac. Event data is kept in memory to show the list and schedule local reminders.

Nothing shared, nothing tracked

No analytics, no advertising, and no third party receives your leave or calendar data. Read the full privacy policy for the details.

Up and running in a few minutes.

Kakitangan Companion is a signed and notarized installer for macOS 12 or later.

1

Install

Download the latest .pkg from GitHub Releases and run it. Later versions update from inside the app.

2

Allow notifications

Click Allow notifications on the setup screen, then enable Time Sensitive alerts in System Settings so reminders break through Focus.

3

Connect

Add your Kakitangan account and the people you want to monitor. Connect Google when you want meetings in the same window.
